NagarSathi

NagarSathi is an AI-powered municipal assistant that helps citizens find information about municipal services using official government documents.

Instead of searching through many documents, citizens can simply ask a question in normal language. NagarSathi finds relevant information from the available municipal documents and provides a clear answer with references.

What NagarSathi Can Help With

NagarSathi can answer questions related to municipal services such as:

🏠 Property tax
🏗️ Building permission
📜 Trade license
⚡ Electricity connection
♻️ Sanitation
🎂 Birth certificate
And other municipal services covered by the available documents
How It Works
The citizen asks a question.
NagarSathi searches the available municipal documents.
Relevant information is retrieved.
The AI generates an answer based on those documents.
References are provided so the information can be checked.

The goal is to keep answers simple, useful, and based on official municipal information.

Important Note

NagarSathi provides information based on the municipal documents available to it.

All answers are based only on official municipal documents.

If the required information is not available in those documents, NagarSathi will let the user know.

AI-generated information may contain mistakes. Please verify important information with the relevant municipal authority or official document before taking action.

Purpose

The purpose of NagarSathi is to make municipal information easier for citizens to understand and access.

It is designed to reduce the time people spend searching through government documents and help them find the information they need in a simpler way.
